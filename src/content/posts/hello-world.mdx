## Hey there

I'm Jacopo — a **Senior IT & Security Engineer** based in Japan, with **15+ years** across cloud security, identity & access management, and IT governance. I work with startups and growing organizations as a remote contractor, turning reactive IT operations into proactive, product-minded engineering.

Most recently that's meant securing distributed teams with **Zero Trust** (Cloudflare), enforcing least privilege at scale with **Terraform** on **AWS**, and automating security workflows in **Python** across Okta, Google Workspace, and the rest of the SaaS stack. I'm equally comfortable owning infrastructure end to end and mapping it to frameworks like **PCI-DSS** and **NIST**.

Right now I'm contracting at **Meedan** — auditing services, rightsizing cloud spend, and building **[catalogIT](https://github.com/jonymaster/catalogIT)**, an open-source asset catalog that replaced fragmented spreadsheets and SnipeIT. I'm also retained by **Welltech** as an Atlassian consultant after helping them scale their IT & Security function, migrate the org to Jira, and get audit-ready. Before that, I spent four years at **Glovo** as a Platform Security Engineer, leading the move from legacy VPN to identity-based access for thousands of endpoints.

This blog is where I'll share what I learn along the way: practical tips, project write-ups, lessons from the field, and the occasional deep-dive into something that caught my attention.

## What to expect

- **Security engineering** — Zero Trust, IAM (Okta, Entra ID, AWS), compliance readiness, and automation that scales beyond a checklist.
- **Atlassian administration** — advanced Jira and Confluence workflows, migrations, governance, and the kind of automation that survives rapid growth.
- **Infrastructure & DevOps** — Terraform, cloud posture, MDM, observability, and the glue between on-prem and cloud.
- **IT as a product** — vendor consolidation, identity lifecycle, internal tooling (like catalogIT), and shifting IT from ticket queues to engineering.
- **AI in the stack** — how I use AI tools and platforms in day-to-day engineering work without treating them as magic.

If any of that sounds useful, stick around. More posts coming soon.
