Three things that genuinely change

Not everything can move across unchanged, and in a couple of places we deliberately chose not to.

Status lives in one place now

In Asana, a link's state was recorded twice — once in a status field and once by which column it sat in — and the two often disagreed. We used your own status mapping to settle it, with one rule: a card in an archive or completed column is finished, whatever its field said. So some records will show a tidier status in Jira than they did in Asana. That is deliberate, and from now on there is only one place to set it.

Duplicates are merged

709 tasks lived on two boards at once and would have arrived twice. They are now single records, filed under the product they belong to.

Recurring work is automatic

Asana created the next copy of a recurring task for you. Jira has no such thing built in, so we built it. Set a task's Recurrence — weekly, monthly, quarterly — and give it a due date. Complete it on or before that date and the next one appears automatically with the next due date. Miss the date and nothing is generated: the task gets a comment telling you the schedule has paused, and you restart it by setting a new due date. We found 23 recurring routines in your boards and have carried them over with the cadence they were actually running at.

What we need from you

One pass through your own product's project by Friday 21 August, looking for things only you would notice. Half an hour of your attention this week is worth far more than a month of corrections afterwards — and after the 28th the Asana original is gone, so this is the window.

  • Are your links the right type? Open a handful you know well. A link should be a Link, a test should be an Experiment. If something is filed as a plain Task that shouldn't be, tell us.
  • Do the statuses look right? Especially anything you'd call live, paused or stopped. This is where Asana disagreed with itself most often.
  • Are experiments attached to the correct link? Open a link and check the experiments listed under it are really its own.
  • Are you the owner where you should be? And is anyone assigned work that isn't theirs?
  • Is anything important missing? A link or test you'd expect to find and can't.

Send anything you find to Jacopo by Friday 21 August. That leaves the following week to correct the rules and re-test before cutover. Concrete is better than general — an issue key or a link name we can look up beats “the statuses seem off”.

One thing we can't fully carry

A small number of very old records — under 4% of the total, almost all completed and archived years ago — sat underneath parent tasks that live on boards outside this migration, sometimes on the personal task list of someone who has since left. Where a parent isn't coming, its sub-items can't be placed correctly, so those arrive without their original nesting or don't arrive at all.

Everything current is covered. We chased this deliberately rather than discovering it later: it started at around 15% of the data and we recovered most of it by repairing the connections first. What remains is the residue, and it is old.
